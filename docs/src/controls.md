# Keys and shortcuts

Everything in Rondelek works with the mouse. These keys are there for anyone
who likes the keyboard better.

## On the sound board

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| `1 2 3 4` / `Q W E R` / `A S D F` | the twelve keys, row by row |
| `Space` | switch between recording and playing (the **REC** key) |
| *(recording)* hold a key | record while it's held; let go or press `Esc` to stop |
| *(playing)* tap a key | play its sound |

The square key just left of **REC** changes the little screen: sound bars,
vowels, or off.

## During voice calibration

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| hold `Space` | record the vowel on screen, just like holding the big button |

## In the games

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| `A E I O U Y` | acts like saying that vowel (handy for trying a game out) |
| `Esc` | end the game and go back to the app |

## Anywhere

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| `F12` | open or close the **For grown-ups** page |
| `Ctrl+Shift+S` | save a screenshot (on the sound board, into that session's folder) |
