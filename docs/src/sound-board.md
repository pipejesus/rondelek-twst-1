# The sound board

The sound board is Rondelek's first way to play, and the one that works right
away, even before the voice calibration. Twelve big keys, and every one can
hold a sound: a giggle, a growl, a sung *"la-la-la"*, a dog's bark, a
grandparent saying hello. Record it, tap it, and there it is again.

Open it with the **Sounds** tile on your child's screen.

## Recording a sound

1. Press the red **REC** key at the top right (or the `Space` bar). The board
   switches to recording mode.
2. **Hold a key** and make the sound. It records for as long as you hold it.
3. Let go (or press `Esc`) to stop.

A little light in the key's corner shows that it now holds a sound. Recording
onto a key that already has one simply replaces it.

## Playing the sounds

Press **REC** (or `Space`) again to get back to playing, then **tap** any key
to hear its sound. Tap as often as you like, and try several keys quickly:
the sounds play on top of each other, so a child can make their own little
song out of them.

Each key has a letter on it, matching the keyboard: **1 2 3 4** on the top
row, **Q W E R** in the middle and **A S D F** at the bottom. So the sound
board works with the mouse or the keyboard, whichever your child likes best.

## The little screen

Above the keys is a small glowing screen. The square key just left of **REC**
changes what it shows:

- **Sound bars**: little bars that dance along with every sound, whether it's
  your child's voice or a key playing back.
- **Vowels**: a big letter showing which vowel the app hears, with a meter for
  each of the six. This one needs the [voice calibration](voice-calibration.md),
  and it's a lovely way to check that the calibration worked.
- **Off**: a calm, dark screen.

## Everything is kept

You never need to save. Every recording is kept straight away, and the next
time your child opens **Sounds**, their board is exactly as they left it.

Each set of twelve recordings is called a **session**. Now and then it's nice
to start fresh, say on a new day or for a new game idea. On your child's
screen, **Start with empty pads** begins a new, empty session. The old one
isn't gone: grown-ups can open any earlier session again from the
[grown-ups page](grown-ups.md#sessions).

To leave the sound board, press the back key at the top left.
